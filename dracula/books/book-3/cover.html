“My revenge is just begun! I spread it over centuries, and time is on my side.”
Count Dracula
Lucy is at rest, and the men who loved her have sworn to hunt down the thing that killed her. Mina types the whole record of the horror into one dossier; they search London for the Count’s fifty boxes of earth and sterilise them one by one. Then the Count comes for Mina, and when Van Helsing touches her brow with the sacred wafer, it burns a scar into her forehead. From her trances they follow him by sea, by rail and by river, back across Europe to his castle, racing the winter sunset.
Book III of three: chapters XVII–XXVII of Bram Stoker’s novel of 1897, and the closing Note, read by a cast drawn from the Amenti Library of Legends, with paintings, music and the sounds of London, the sea and the Carpathians.
