# Dracula · Book II Build Document

24 October 2026 · Ian Ingram

## Status

Book II (Lucy) is finished but not finalized: fully illustrated, scored and live as its own page, with its cover chosen (Option A, Lucy at the gate). Nothing changes until it is finalized.

- **Finished** = every chapter reads, plays and is illustrated, with no scene holding one picture much past a minute.
- **Finalized** = Ian has watched it through in Safari, signed off, and it has an ISBN and print-ready art.
- **After finalizing**, the digital stamps go on: chapter QR codes and the Ask Amenti QR, as planned for Book I.

## What Book II is

Chapters V–XVI of Stoker's *Dracula*, told in letters, journals and diaries, played straight through in three sections.

| Section | Title | Chapters | Card painting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Whitby | V–VIII | Whitby from the cliff by day |
| II | The Blood | IX–XII | Sunset |
| III | The Un-Dead | XIII–XVI | The heath at night |

Live page: [Dracula · Book II · Lucy](https://ianingram.github.io/Amenti-Studios/dracula/part-2/) — a title film, a section card before chapters V, IX and XIII, a painted card before every chapter, and an ending that turns to Book III, The Hunt. `?end` opens straight on the ending.

## What is built

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Reading | Chapters V–XVI as cue sheets (`ep05`–`ep16.json`), every line voiced by its cast member |
| Paintings | About 470 Midjourney paintings by Ian, about one a minute; cast reference faces made first (`art/ref/`), hands checked at full size before placing |
| Colour | Whitby's last fine day in rich colour, sea fog in the storm, Lucy in the gardens; darker as the book turns |
| The Demeter | Ian's giant-sun painting at Whitby opens chapter VII; the storm plays as a moving picture |
| The moon | Follows the real September 1893 sky in XIII–XVI (full moon 25 Sept) |
| Lucy | Sleepwalking set for VIII–IX; backlit set for her return un-dead in XVI; the crucifix scene redone |
| Van Helsing | Clean-shaven as Stoker has him, alpine glasses and a leather overcoat, locked with one reference portrait |
| Cards | 3 section cards and 12 chapter cards in Book I style (`part-2/cards/`) |
| Title film | ~57 s: Whitby by day, the pier, the sunset Demeter, the storm, Lucy at the gate, the Count in Piccadilly, Lucy un-dead |
| Ending | "And then begins our great quest" over the oath, the credit roll, then Book III · The Hunt · to come |
| Cover | `cover.html` in this folder: front A (Lucy at the gate, moon cross), spine, back (Lucy backlit, "Come to me, Arthur…", blurb, cast) |
